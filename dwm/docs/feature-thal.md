# Feature: Thal

See also: [Docs Index](./index.md), [Skaro Dimension](./feature-skaro-dimension.md), [Dalek](./feature-dalek.md), [Entity Definitions](./feature-entity-definitions.md)

## Product Intent
Give Skaro a peaceful, living population to contrast with the Dalek patrols: a neutral Thal who wanders, greets players with a glance, and runs from Daleks. This pass ships the mob itself (DWM-071, entity slice); Thals reach the world through plateau settlements added separately, not through biome spawning.

## Player Outcomes
- Spawn a Thal from the **Thal Spawn Egg** (Spawn Eggs tab, right after the Time Lord) or with `/summon dwm:thal`.
- Four cosmetic looks (`thal_1` to `thal_4`) are picked at random on spawn and kept when the world is saved and reloaded.
- A Thal wanders, opens and closes doors behind it, and turns its head toward nearby players.
- When hurt it panics and runs; it never fights back and never targets players.
- Within 12 blocks of a Dalek, Paradigm Dalek, or Dalek mutant it flees, walking when the threat is far and sprinting when it is close.

## Behaviour
| Aspect | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Entity | `dwm:thal`, `MobCategory.CREATURE`, 0.6 x 1.95 hitbox |
| Stats | 20 max health, 0.3 movement speed (`data/dwm/entity/definition/thal.json`) |
| Float | Swims up when in water |
| Avoid | `dwm:avoid_entity` on `#dwm:thal_fears`, 12 blocks, walk 1.0 / sprint 1.3 |
| Panic | Speed 1.25 when hurt |
| Doors | Opens and closes doors |
| Wander | Water-avoiding stroll at speed 1.0 |
| Look | Looks at players within 8 blocks, plus random look-around |
| Targets | None (the definition has no `targets`) |
| Loot | Empty table |
| Sounds | Villager-style ambient / hurt / death with subtitles ("Thal murmurs / hurts / dies") |

The variant is a synced entity datum (vanilla tracking only, no custom packets) saved as the NBT string `Variant`. Unknown ids fall back to `thal_1`. Set one explicitly with `/summon dwm:thal ~ ~ ~ {Variant:"thal_3"}`.

## Fear tag: `#dwm:thal_fears`
The set of things a Thal runs from is the entity-type tag `dwm:thal_fears`, generated by `DWMEntityTagProvider` from `DWMEntityTypeTags.THAL_FEARS`. It contains `dwm:dalek`, `dwm:paradigm_dalek`, and `dwm:dalek_mutant`.

To make Thals flee something else (for example a future Slyther), add its entity type to that tag in `DWMEntityTagProvider` and run `./gradlew runDatagen`; no goal or JSON change is needed. A datapack can also extend the tag, since it is an ordinary entity-type tag.

## Spawning
- **No natural or biome spawning.** No biome lists `dwm:thal` as a spawner (guarded by `ResourceValidationTests.noBiomeSpawnsThals`). Residents come only from plateau settlements.
- The spawn placement predicate delegates to `ThalSpawnRules`: `NATURAL` and `CHUNK_GENERATION` spawns are rejected unless the position is inside a Thal settlement; every other reason (spawn egg, `/summon`, structure, triggered, ...) is allowed when the vanilla mob spawn rules pass.
- `ThalSpawnRules.isInsideSettlement` currently always returns `false`. The settlement structure lookup is plugged in there when the plateau settlement ships.

## Not Included
- No trades, professions, or merchant screen.
- No dialogue, quests, or reputation.
- No breeding or growth stages (Thals are not `Animal`s).
- No unique drops.
- No Dalek hostility toward Thals yet (see [Dalek](./feature-dalek.md)); Thals only run.

## Known Constraints
- Stats and AI are JSON-authored; edits need a game restart, not `/reload` (see [Entity Definitions](./feature-entity-definitions.md)).
- Thals have no settlement to live in yet, so outside spawn eggs and commands they do not appear in the world.

## Testing
- Unit: `ThalEntityTest`, `ThalSpawnRulesTest`, `EntityDefinitionCatalogTest` (shipped stats, `dwm:avoid_entity` parsing), and resource checks in `ResourceValidationTests` (textures, spawn egg model, `thal_fears` tag, no biome spawns).
- GameTests (`ThalGameTests`): matches its definition, variant survives save and load, flees a Dalek, panics rather than targets when hit by a player, and natural spawns are rejected while spawn egg and command spawns are allowed.
- Client GameTest: `entities_thals` screenshot of the four variants.
